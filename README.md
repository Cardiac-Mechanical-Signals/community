# Cardiac Mechanical Signals

**An open community for researchers studying the mechanical activity of the heart.**

Cardiac Mechanical Signals brings together researchers, clinicians, engineers, and students working with ballistocardiography (BCG), seismocardiography (SCG), phonocardiography (PCG) and related measurements of cardiac mechanical activity.

Our goals are to connect people across these fields, share recent work, and develop common frameworks that make research easier to compare, reproduce, and build upon.

## What we do

- **Discuss research:** Share ideas, questions, results, and challenges with researchers around the world.
- **Follow the field:** Highlight new publications, datasets, tools, events, and opportunities for collaboration.
- **Build a shared literature collection:** Maintain a community-curated catalog of relevant papers with citations and links.
- **Develop common practices:** Work toward shared terminology, measurement and annotation methods, reporting guidance, and evaluation frameworks.

This community welcomes contributions across signal types, sensing technologies, clinical applications, and stages of research.

## Join the conversation

Visit the **Discussions** tab to introduce yourself, ask a question, share a finding, or suggest a topic for the group. All constructive contributions are welcome, including early ideas and questions from people new to the field.

When sharing a new publication, please include its title, authors, publication year, DOI or stable link, and a short explanation of why it is relevant.

## Papers and resources

The **`papers`** repository will hold a curated catalog of publications and useful resources. Researchers can suggest additions through Discussions or propose edits to the repository. Please link to a publisher page, DOI, preprint, or authorized open-access version rather than uploading a copyrighted PDF.

## Community frameworks

The **`frameworks`** repository will hold proposals and evolving documents on topics such as signal definitions, acquisition, preprocessing, annotation, quality assessment, analysis, and reporting. We encourage open discussion before changes are incorporated. Early documents represent **draft community recommendations**; they should not be described as formal standards without an agreed approval process.

## Our approach

We aim for respectful, inclusive, and evidence-based discussion. Different methods and interpretations are welcome. Please identify potential limitations, cite relevant work where possible, and give proper credit to contributors.

**Whether you work on BCG, SCG, PCG, or another cardiac mechanical signal, we invite you to help shape this community.**
